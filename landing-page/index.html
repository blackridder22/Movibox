A few things worth knowing.

What is MoviBox?

MoviBox is a desktop app for discovering, downloading, and organizing movies and series from your configured sources. It brings Stremio-compatible add-ons, source selection, a download queue, and episode monitoring into one place.

Is it really free?

Yes. MoviBox is free and its source code is available under the MIT license. Optional third-party services, such as TorBox or other debrid providers, have their own pricing and account requirements.

Does it include movies or streaming subscriptions?

No. MoviBox does not supply content, bundled content add-ons, or streaming subscriptions. You connect your own compatible add-ons and providers. Only download files you have permission to obtain.

Can I watch inside MoviBox?

MoviBox focuses on downloads. Completed files open in the external app you choose, such as IINA, VLC, or Infuse. You can also add your files to a Plex or Jellyfin library.

How does automatic downloading work?

Choose a series, select seasons and a quality profile, then enable future-episode monitoring. MoviBox checks for released episodes, skips files already queued or downloaded, and queues matching sources. Your computer must be awake and MoviBox running.

Do I need Sonarr, Radarr, or Prowlarr?

No. MoviBox works through your Stremio-compatible sources and configured download providers. It does not require the Servarr apps. If you already use those tools, you can try MoviBox independently without migrating that setup.
