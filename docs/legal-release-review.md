## Proposed legal package

- Keep the standard MIT license and its warranty/liability language. Preserve Harbor's existing notice and add blackridder22 for MoviBox modifications.
- Add `USE_NOTICE.md` for lawful use, privacy and account abuse, service quotas and account suspension, background activity, third-party independence, modified builds, and risks.
- Finalize the draft `PRIVACY.md` with the maintainer's public contact, country, effective date, applicable legal basis, rights route, and actual retention practice. It describes Tally and Airtable separately from local app data.
- Retain dependency and asset notices. `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` is an overview, not a completed dependency-license audit.
- Make these documents accessible in Settings and the installer before the public release. Replace the existing installer text with a notice consistent with MIT rather than adding contradictory software-use restrictions.

This is a technical and drafting review, not a legal opinion. The applicability and enforceability of disclaimers depend on jurisdiction and facts. A locally qualified lawyer should review the final documents if the maintainer needs legal assurance.

## Sources reviewed

- [MIT license](https://opensource.org/license/mit) and [OSI explanation of restrictions on use](https://opensource.org/faq): MIT permits modification and redistribution; adding a prohibition on categories of users or purposes changes the licensing proposition.
- [Apple Keychain permission guidance](https://support.apple.com/guide/keychain-access/if-youre-asked-for-access-to-your-keychain-kyca1243/mac): a permission prompt is not determined solely by debug versus release mode.
- [TorBox terms](https://www.torbox.app/terms), [acceptable use](https://torbox.app/policies/acceptable-use), and [Real-Debrid terms](https://real-debrid.com/terms): provider restrictions and suspension risks are controlled by those services.
- [ICO privacy-notice information guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/): useful drafting checklist; applicability and the final legal basis depend on the maintainer's circumstances.
- [Tally privacy policy](https://tally.so/help/privacy-policy) and [Airtable privacy policy](https://www.airtable.com/company/privacy): third-party handling must be distinguished from MoviBox's local storage.
